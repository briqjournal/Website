Don’t Forget While Fighting for Peace (1962)*
It is a poster series titled “Boryas Za Mir - Ne Zabıvay” (Don’t Forget While Fighting for Peace)
by Viktor Borisovich Koretskiy (1909-1998), who created the most famous Soviet posters.
This series of posters on the theme of peace and the struggle for
independence was published in Moscow in 1962.
