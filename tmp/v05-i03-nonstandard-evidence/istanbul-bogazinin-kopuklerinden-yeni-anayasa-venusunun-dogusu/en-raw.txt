                                         SATIRIKON – 1908/16




              The Birth of the Venus of the New Constitution from the Foam of the Bosphorus*

      In the subtitle on the cover of the 16th issue of the Satirikon weekly humor and satirical magazine published
      in St. Petersburg, the capital of Tsarist Russia, in 1908, the epigram read: “Abdulhamit: Illallah! Here is the
                                    only woman I would not want to see in my harem.”




      **[Cartoon]. (1908). The Birth of the Venus of the New Constitution from the Foam of the Bosphorus. Satirikon, 16.
