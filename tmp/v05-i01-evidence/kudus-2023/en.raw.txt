HEBA ZAGOUT
*80x60cm, Acrylic on canvas.
Palestinian artist, Heba Zaqout, was born, studied and lived in Gaza. And Heba Zagout was martyred along with her
children in latest Israel’s assault against Gaza. In her paintings she depicted mostly Palestinian houses, minarets, domes
and churches to emphasize the Palestinian identity and Palestinian existence. Zaqout's unique style and riot of colours
are immediately evident in her paintings, where ethnic elements stand out. Her devotion to her hometown and country
is like the starting point of her paintings.
